## Order of attack

**Days 1–3: build the portfolio first.** Every application asks for samples.
- Film **3 sample videos** in the study/app niche: one hook-driven "this app saved my exam" style video, one tutorial or demo, and one talking-head reaction.
- Make a new TikTok account for them (for example `@[you]studies`). Pin all three.

**Day 3: apply to everything in Tier 1** and log it in `tracker.csv`.

**Day 3 onward: start Whop Content Rewards.** You get small but real pay and practice while you wait for replies.

**Week 2: set up Tier 3 profiles** (JoinBrands and SideShift first) so the rest of your week is full.

**Weeks 3–4: trials.** Programs keep the creators whose trial videos get views. Log each video's hook and 48-hour views in the tracker so you learn which hooks work.

**Target:** 1–2 Tier 1 programs keep you by week 4, and **$500–1,000/month by day 60–90**.

---

## Red flags: walk away if
- They ask **you** to pay (for "training", "onboarding" or a "starter kit").
- They won't say the pay until after you've filmed.
- There's no written terms or contract.
- They want your login details for an account.
- They want "AI videos of you", or you to present AI-generated people as real.

**Always:**
- Tag videos #ad, or use the platform's paid-partnership toggle.
- Keep records for taxes. This is self-employment income.
