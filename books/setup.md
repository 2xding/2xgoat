Work through this top to bottom. Tick boxes as you go.

- [ ] **TikTok:** `@reads.withtheo`

**TikTok link rules:** personal accounts usually need about **1,000 followers** before they can add a website link. Until then:
- put the link in your **Instagram** bio and write "link on my IG" in your TikTok bio, and
- pin a comment: "tracker link is on my IG 📚".

**Stay on a personal account, not Business.** Business accounts lose most trending sounds, and sounds help slideshows get shown.

## Warm-up (days 1–3, ~15 min/day)
- Follow 20–30 book and self-improvement accounts. Like and save posts in the niche.
- Leave 5 genuine comments a day on big book posts. Early, thoughtful comments get people to click your profile.
- This teaches TikTok who to show your posts to.

## Posting
- Upload in **Photo mode**: select all PNGs from `books/out/<post>/` in order.
- Add a **trending or chill instrumental sound** at low volume.
- Paste the `caption` from `posts.json`. Add the **AI-generated label** only if AI made the images. These are designed text slides, so it isn't needed.
- Best times to test: **7–9am, 12–1pm, 7–10pm** (your time zone). Post 1–2 per day.

## Track results (every post, after 48h)
Log **views, likes, saves, shares, comments, profile visits**. Saves and shares matter most. After ~14 posts, keep the 2 best formats and drop the worst.

## Rules
- Only claims that are actually in the books. Check stats before posting.
- Label affiliate links ("affiliate link" / #ad in captions or emails where you use them).
- Don't buy followers or use engagement pods.
